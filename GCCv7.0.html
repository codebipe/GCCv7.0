How Final Cost Is Calculated

  1. Determine green cost per lb from price per lb, or from price per container / container lbs.
  2. Adjust for roast loss: roasted cost per lb = green cost per lb / yield factor.
  3. Add packaging allocation per lb from your Packaging tab values.
  4. Add overhead allocation per lb from your Overhead tab values.
  5. Final cost per lb = roasted cost per lb + packaging + overhead.
